## 📌 Executive Summary

A predictive healthcare data analytics platform developed for Apollo Hospital operations. The project combines exploratory patient data analysis, emergency ward load tracking, disease trend mapping, and machine learning time-series forecasting to predict future bed occupancy and medical supply requirements.

---

## 💡 Key Features & Insights

- **🚑 Patient Admission Forecasting:** Predictive ML modeling forecasting weekly patient inflows to prevent hospital bottlenecks.
- **🩺 Disease & Diagnosis Trends:** Interactive tracking of seasonal illness surges and length-of-stay (LOS) distributions.
- **💊 Hospital Resource Allocation:** Optimization metrics for ICU beds, ventilator availability, and medical staff shifts.

- **Machine Learning & EDA:** Python (`Pandas`, `NumPy`, `Seaborn`, `Scikit-Learn`)
- **Business Intelligence:** Power BI Interactive Dashboards
